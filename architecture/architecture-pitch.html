LEGEND — AI vs DETERMINISTIC
AI · probabilistic — a model's output, can be wrong
Deterministic — fixed logic, same input → same output
Teal ring = defensible IP
Dashed = policy / note

Only speech-to-text and the extractor are probabilistic — and the extractor is extraction only, no clinical reasoning, so its one job is filling a schema, not deciding anything.

Everything that ranks, scores, or flags is deterministic: fixed, versioned severity constants, auditable feature-matching, <50ms, zero model calls — DXplain lineage, running since 1987.

The AI reads the room, the math decides the ranking — a doctor can audit why something was ranked without trusting an LLM's judgment call.
